### What does it mean to partner with Squarespace?

A partnership is a collaboration that helps people discover, use, or get more value from Squarespace products. Depending on your organization and goals, that could mean building an app or integration, reselling eligible products, referring new customers, or working with Squarespace through a professional partner program.

### What is a reseller partner?

A reseller partner offers eligible Squarespace products to its own customers, often as part of its existing service or customer experience. Depending on the arrangement, a partner may integrate with Squarespace’s Reseller API or use another approved referral or sales path. Reseller partners don’t need to become accredited domain registrars themselves, but they must be approved and operate under the applicable program terms. Learn about domain reselling.

### What is an app or extension partner?

An app or extension partner builds a third-party tool that connects with Squarespace to help customers run or grow their websites and businesses. Some integrations may be eligible for inclusion in one of our directories, such as Squarespace Extensions or Acuity Integrations. A directory listing is subject to applicable review and approval; submitting an inquiry does not guarantee acceptance or listing.

### What is an integrated partner?

“Integrated partner” describes a partner whose product or service connects with Squarespace—for example, through an API or an extension. It describes the nature of the technical connection, not one universal partner program. An integration may or may not also be listed in one of our directories, involve a reseller relationship, or include a commercial agreement.

### What is an affiliate partner?

An affiliate partner promotes Squarespace using tracked links. If a referred, first-time customer makes a qualifying purchase, an approved affiliate may earn a commission under the Affiliate Program’s terms. Applications are reviewed, and payout details are provided through the program. Explore the Squarespace Affiliate Program.

### Is Squarespace Circle the same as the Affiliate Program?

No. Squarespace Circle is a partner program for freelancers and agencies who build websites for clients on Squarespace. Circle offers program benefits for eligible members; referral payments require separate registration. The Affiliate Program is a separate application-based program for partners who refer new customers through tracked links. Compare the programs and explore Circle.

### Can a partner participate in more than one type of partnership?

Potentially. These descriptions aren’t necessarily exclusive: for example, a company could build an integration and also have a separate reseller or referral arrangement. Each activity is subject to its own eligibility, review, and terms. The Squarespace team can help identify the right path.

### Which partnership path is right for my organization?

- Choose an integration path if you have a product that could connect to Squarespace and benefit customers.
- Explore reselling if you want to offer eligible Squarespace products within your own customer experience.
- Explore affiliate if you have an audience and want to refer prospective customers using tracked links.
- Explore Squarespace Circle if you build Squarespace websites for clients.
- If your idea doesn’t fit neatly into one of these paths, tell us about it through the partner inquiry form.

### What are the benefits of partnering with Squarespace?

Benefits depend on the partnership type and any applicable approval or agreement. Depending on the path, a partnership may offer product distribution or discovery, a way to serve shared customers, referral or reseller earnings, technical resources, or marketing opportunities. No benefit, listing, payment, or level of support is guaranteed unless it is included in the relevant program terms or agreement.

### Are there fees, commissions, or revenue-sharing terms?

There is no single commercial model for every partnership. Affiliate commissions, reseller economics, app pricing, and any other commercial terms are determined by the applicable program terms or a separate agreement. Review the terms for your specific program before participating. Squarespace Circle is free to join; other partnership paths may have different requirements or terms.

### Do I need to be an existing Squarespace customer?

It depends on the program. Squarespace Circle is for Squarespace customers who build websites for clients. Other partner paths have their own requirements; check the relevant program information or share your proposal with the partner team.

### Does submitting an inquiry guarantee approval, an integration, or an Extensions listing?

No. Each opportunity is evaluated for fit, customer value, technical and operational readiness, and applicable program requirements. Approval to explore a partnership does not by itself guarantee a contract, API access, launch, directory listing, or marketing support.

### What technical or review steps should I expect?

For integrations, the steps depend on the APIs, permissions, and product experience involved. Partners should review the applicable developer documentation and terms before building. Additional technical, security, privacy, product, or listing reviews may apply.

### How long does it take to become a partner?

There isn’t one timeline for every partner type. Timing depends on the program, the information provided, technical scope, and any required reviews or agreements. The partner team can discuss likely next steps after learning more about your proposal.

### Where can I find partner resources?

- Apps and extensions: Browse the Extensions directory and visit the Squarespace Developer site for API documentation.

### How do I get started or share a partnership idea?

Choose the program page above if you already know which path fits. Otherwise, tell us about your organization, product or audience, the customers you serve, and what you’d like to accomplish with Squarespace using the Partner inquiry form. We’ll route your inquiry to the appropriate team.
