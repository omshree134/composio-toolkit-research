# Firecrawl Pricing

Start for free, then scale as you grow.

## Plans

### Free

- Credits: 1,000 per month
- Price: $0 (no credit card required)
- Concurrency: 2 concurrent requests
- Rate limits: Low rate limits

### Hobby

- Credits: 5,000 per month
- Price: $19/month or $16/month billed annually ($ 190/year, save $38)
- Concurrency: 5 concurrent requests
- Support: Basic support

### Standard (Recommended)

- Credits: 100,000 per month
- Price: $99/month or $83/month billed annually ($ 990/year, save $198)
- Concurrency: 25 concurrent requests
- Support: Standard support

### Growth

- Credits: 500,000 per month
- Price: $399/month or $333/month billed annually ($ 3,990/year, save $798)
- Concurrency: 50 concurrent requests
- Support: Priority support

### Scale

- Concurrency: 100 concurrent requests

- Concurrent browsers: Concurrent browsers set how many pages render at the same time.
- Max queued jobs: This is how many jobs can wait in the queue at once. Above Scale you get 2,000 for each browser, up to 2,000,000.
- Rate limit, /scrape, /map and /search: Your team gets this many requests a minute for each endpoint.
- Rate limit, /crawl and /agent: Your team can send this many requests a minute. Batch scrape shares the crawl limit, and extract shares the agent limit.
- Rate limit, /interact: Your team can start this many browser sandbox sessions a minute.

## Billing FAQ

Is Firecrawl free? Yes. Firecrawl gives you 1,000 free credits every month, which covers about 1,000 pages, and no card is required. When you need more, choose Hobby, Standard, Growth, or Scale. Every paid plan runs on pay-as-you-go: you get the plan's monthly credits, and we add more in 5 USD increments when you run out. You set a monthly limit to cap the spend, or turn pay-as-you-go off. AI agents can get started with the onboarding skill at https://www.firecrawl.dev/agent-onboarding/SKILL.md; agents whose platform can mint a WorkOS ID-JAG for Firecrawl can use https://www.firecrawl.dev/auth.md for direct authentication.

Is there a pay-per-use plan? Yes. Pay-as-you-go is how self-serve billing works. You start with 1,000 free credits every month. On Hobby, Standard, Growth, or Scale you get that plan's monthly credits, and we add more in 5 USD increments when you run out. A 5 USD increment is 1,000 credits on Hobby, 2,000 on Standard, 2,500 on Growth, and 5,000 on Scale. Pay-as-you-go needs a paid plan. You set a monthly limit to cap the spend, and you can buy credits yourself at any time.

Do credits roll over to the next month? Credits roll over on Scale and Enterprise. Scale carries unused credits for one month, and Enterprise carries a custom amount. Hobby, Standard and Growth credits do not roll over.

Do pay-as-you-go credits expire? Credits you buy stay on your account until you use them. They expire if you cancel your subscription, so use them before you leave.

How does pay-as-you-go work? We add credits to your account when your balance runs out, so your requests keep working. It is for paid plans only. Each increment costs 5 USD and charges the card on file. Set your Monthly pay-as-you-go limit in your Billing settings: enter a whole multiple of 5 USD to cap the monthly spend, enter 0 to turn pay-as-you-go off, or enter nothing for no limit. Credits you buy yourself do not count toward that limit.

How many credits does a 5 USD pay-as-you-go increment give me? A 5 USD increment gives you 1,000 credits on Hobby, 2,000 on Standard, 2,500 on Growth, and 5,000 on Scale.

How many credits does each request cost? Scrape and Crawl each cost 1 credit per page, Map costs 1 credit per call, and Monitor costs 1 credit per page per check. Search costs 2 credits per 10 results. Interact costs 2 credits per browser minute. The Firecrawl Research Index paper endpoints are free across every category, including AI/ML and life sciences. Agent is in preview with 5 free daily runs and dynamic pricing. Advanced features (JSON format, etc.) cost additional credits. See the credits table above for more details.

Do you charge for failed requests? A scrape that returns no result is not charged. A page that responds with an error status such as 403 or 404 is still returned to you and costs 1 credit. If a charge looks incorrect, reach out to help@firecrawl.com and we'll take a look.

What payment methods do you accept? Payments through Stripe, which accepts most major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal.

Can I buy Firecrawl through AWS Marketplace or Google Cloud Marketplace? Yes. Firecrawl is sold on AWS Marketplace and Google Cloud Marketplace, so the charges go on your AWS or Google Cloud bill. On Google Cloud, a private offer also draws down your Google Cloud spend commitment. Contact sales for a private offer with custom pricing and terms.

Why am I being charged VAT (or any local tax) even though I provided a tax ID? Local tax is applied based on your location and local tax rules, and it's calculated automatically. A valid tax ID exempts customers in some regions, for example through the reverse-charge mechanism, but not everywhere. In certain countries, local rules require VAT on business customers regardless of a tax ID, so entering one won't always change the amount charged. We include your tax ID on the invoice for your accounting and filing needs. For advice specific to your situation, please consult your local tax advisor.
