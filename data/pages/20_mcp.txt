# Set up the Gladly MCP for Claude

Connect Claude to your Gladly organization so you can investigate sessions, improve Guides and Workflows, and test changes in plain English. Setup takes about 5 minutes and doesn't require any downloads. To learn what the Gladly MCP for Claude does, see [What is the Gladly MCP for Claude?](https://help.gladly.com/docs/what-is-the-gladly-mcp-for-claude)

**Your Claude screens may look different** Claude updates its interface often, so menu names, button labels, and screens may not match the steps and images in this article. The steps below describe what to look for, not exactly where to click. If you get stuck, search Claude's Settings for **Plugins** or **Connectors**, or see [Anthropic's Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com).

**MCP is on for your whole organization** Gladly turns MCP on for your organization as a whole, not person by person. Claude gets the same access as the person who connects, so only give eligible roles to people you trust. See [Review security and data for the Gladly MCP for Claude](https://help.gladly.com/docs/review-security-and-data-for-the-gladly-mcp-for-claude).

**Your connection expires after a few days without use** When that happens, Claude asks you to reconnect. Find your Gladly connector in Claude, select **Connect**, and sign in to Gladly again. Your setup stays the same.

**Two different addresses** Setup uses two addresses, and each goes in a different place. The Gladly marketplace address is for adding the Gladly plugin. Your personal connector address is for adding the connector. If you paste the marketplace address where Claude asks for a connector address, Claude can't find the server.

## Before you begin

Make sure all of the following are true:

- Gladly has enabled MCP access for your organization. If you're not sure, ask your Gladly account team.
- You have an eligible role in Gladly: Team Manager, Compliance Admin, or Admin, or a custom role with the Manage Gladly AI, View Gladly AI, or Review Gladly AI permission. Other roles can't connect.
- You use Claude Desktop or claude.ai on a paid plan, and your version allows plugins and custom connectors. If you can't add them, ask your Claude administrator.
- You know the address you use to sign in to Gladly, for example `acme.us-1.gladly.com`.

## Add the Gladly plugin

The Gladly plugin adds the `/gladly-setup` command, which builds your personal connector address and checks your connection. The connector you add next is what gives Claude access to Gladly.

1. In Claude, find where you add plugins. Look for **Plugins** in the sidebar or in Settings.
2. Add a marketplace and enter the Gladly marketplace address:

```plaintext
https://github.com/gladly/claude-plugin
```
3. Confirm the marketplace, then add the **Gladly** plugin from the list. Keep automatic syncing turned on so you get plugin updates as they're released.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/7047b671-c4f2-4df0-bb0a-b9b511fd2452/Images/Documentation/add marketplace.png?sv=2026-02-06&spr=https&st=2026-11-23T12%3A48%3A35Z&se=2026-11-23T13%3A48%3A35Z&sr=b&sp=r&sig=yhEXSj1IlGmwDkJfhRIQorplm8fyBeJa5R63DIcbdTM%3D)

**Start a new chat after you add the plugin** The `/gladly-setup` command isn't available in chats that were open before you added the plugin. Start a new chat before you continue.

## Get your connector address

1. In a new chat, type `/gladly-setup`, or ask Claude to "set up Gladly."
2. Enter the address you use to sign in to Gladly. Claude builds your personal connector address.

| You enter | Claude gives you |
| --- | --- |
| `acme.us-1.gladly.com` | `https://mcp.us-1.gladly.com/acme/mcp` |

If you sign in on an older address without the region, such as [acme.gladly.com](http://acme.gladly.com), enter that instead. Both work.

## Add the connector and sign in

1. In Claude, find where you add connectors. Look for **Connectors** in Settings or on the Plugins page.
2. Add a custom connector. Give it a name you'll recognize, such as **Gladly**, and paste your connector address. ![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/7047b671-c4f2-4df0-bb0a-b9b511fd2452/Images/Documentation/add custom connector.png?sv=2026-02-06&spr=https&st=2026-11-23T12%3A48%3A35Z&se=2026-11-23T13%3A48%3A35Z&sr=b&sp=r&sig=qlCeczZSTR3K%2Fgzv2JGBsgJXUti%2BSN6d5HooDw4Kwh0%3D)
3. Keep the settings Claude detects. You don't need to enter any headers or client details.
4. Select **Connect**. Gladly opens in your browser. Sign in if you're asked.
5. Review what Claude is asking to do, then select **Allow access**.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/7047b671-c4f2-4df0-bb0a-b9b511fd2452/Images/Documentation/gladly access consent.png?sv=2026-02-06&spr=https&st=2026-11-23T12%3A48%3A35Z&se=2026-11-23T13%3A48%3A35Z&sr=b&sp=r&sig=1WcrGc4xd7yRfOtWXtXSDfkbqBUL62ZRGIxUFKgt2D8%3D)

## Confirm that Claude is connected

1. In a new chat, make sure the Gladly connector is turned on. Look for the connector toggle near the message box, and do this in each new chat.
2. Run `/gladly-setup` again, or ask Claude, "Am I connected to Gladly?" Claude should name your organization. Confirm that it's the right one.

If Gladly isn't connected, Claude tells you. See [Troubleshoot the Gladly MCP for Claude](https://help.gladly.com/docs/troubleshoot-the-gladly-mcp-for-claude).

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/7047b671-c4f2-4df0-bb0a-b9b511fd2452/Images/Documentation/gladly setup claude (1).png?sv=2026-02-06&spr=https&st=2026-11-23T12%3A48%3A35Z&se=2026-11-23T13%3A48%3A35Z&sr=b&sp=r&sig=LDkxUbOexChHdzOjPu0fjwtPfCynswjaNKYqRBvLybI%3D)

Once you're connected, see [Use the Gladly MCP for Claude](/product-docs/docs/use-the-gladly-mcp-for-claude) for example prompts.

## Disconnect Claude from Gladly

You can disconnect for yourself, or a Gladly administrator can disconnect access for everyone in your organization.

**Disconnect for yourself** In Claude, find your connectors and remove the Gladly connector.

**Disconnect for everyone in your organization** A Gladly administrator opens **Connected Applications** in Gladly and disconnects the application. Access ends within seconds.
