## Frequently asked questions about the systeme.io MCP server

What is the systeme.io MCP server and how does it help me?

The systeme.io MCP (Model Context Protocol) server is a standard that securely connects your systeme.io account to AI assistants like ChatGPT or Claude. It allows you to interact with your business data using natural language, enabling you to build funnels, manage contacts, and create email campaigns just by typing simple commands in your chat window.

Is it safe to connect my systeme.io account to AI tools?

Yes. We prioritize your security by using a secure authentication process. When you connect via MCP, you are in control of the permissions granted to the AI. You can choose which tools the AI can access (read-only vs. write/delete) and revoke that access at any time directly from your AI provider’s settings.

Do I need to be a developer to use the MCP server?

Not at all. While the MCP standard is built for developers, our direct integration allows you to connect your account to ChatGPT or Claude in just a few clicks. No coding is required; you simply authorize the connection, and you’re ready to start using AI to manage your systeme.io workspace.

Can the AI perform any action on my account?

You are in control. You can configure permission levels for each tool the AI uses: Always allow, Needs approval, or Blocked. For sensitive operations (like creating or deleting contacts, price plans, or coupons), the AI will require your confirmation within the chat before executing any command.

Which systeme.io plans include access to the MCP server?

The MCP server is a powerful feature included in all systeme.io plans. Whether you are on our Free, Startup, Webinar, or Unlimited plan, you can connect your account to AI tools and start leveraging the power of natural language to streamline your marketing and business management.
