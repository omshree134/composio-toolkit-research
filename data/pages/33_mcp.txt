## Which LinkedIn Ads MCP path fits your team?

As of July 13, 2026, LinkedIn has not published an official Ads MCP server. It provides Marketing APIs and an official client library, while community projects and managed services translate those APIs into MCP tools. The right choice depends on who will own access, safeguards, and maintenance.

### LinkedIn Marketing APIs

First-party building blocks, no official MCP

Choose it if

- You have engineers, an approved LinkedIn app, and a narrow custom use case
- You want to control each API request and maintain the agent layer yourself

### Open-source MCPs

Community code with hands-on ownership

Choose it if

- You can evaluate project security, tool coverage, and maintenance activity
- You are prepared to host it and manage LinkedIn authentication

### Markifact

Hosted execution with approval controls

Choose it if

- You manage B2B programs across several accounts or clients
- You need reporting and campaign actions in the same conversation
- You want LinkedIn Ads beside the rest of your marketing stack

## Frequently Asked Questions

Have a different question? Reach out to Markifact support team.

### What is a LinkedIn Ads MCP server? 

A LinkedIn Ads MCP server connects LinkedIn advertising data and actions to AI clients through the Model Context Protocol. With Markifact, you can ask for reports, build campaigns and creatives, resolve B2B targeting, and manage budgets from Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or another MCP client.

### Is this an official LinkedIn MCP server? 

No. As of July 13, 2026, LinkedIn has not published an official MCP server for LinkedIn Ads. LinkedIn provides Marketing APIs and an official Python client for approved developers. Markifact is a third-party managed MCP service that uses LinkedIn's supported API and OAuth access.

### What does LinkedIn offer instead of an official Ads MCP? 

LinkedIn offers Campaign Manager, versioned Marketing APIs, partner programs, and an official API client library. Those are building blocks rather than a ready-to-connect MCP endpoint, so a team using them directly must build the tools, authentication, hosting, safety controls, and maintenance layer.

### Can I use Lead Gen Forms through LinkedIn Ads MCP? 

Yes, with an important boundary. Markifact can attach an existing LinkedIn Lead Gen Form to supported single image, video, and carousel ads, and report on lead performance. The current tool set does not create the form itself inside chat.

### Which LinkedIn ad formats can Markifact create? 

Markifact can create single image, single video, carousel, text, and Dynamic Spotlight ads. It can also list Company Page posts and sponsor an existing post. Format availability still depends on LinkedIn's objective, ad set type, account eligibility, and creative rules.

### Does it support LinkedIn's B2B targeting? 

Yes. You can search LinkedIn targeting facets and resolve valid values for company, job title, seniority, industry, skill, company size, location, school, field of study, and other supported categories before creating an ad set.

### Can I use Matched Audiences and account lists? 

You can use audience identifiers already available to the ad account in campaign targeting, subject to LinkedIn permissions and compatibility. Markifact's current LinkedIn operations do not upload a new company list or create a new Matched Audience from chat, so those source audiences should be prepared in LinkedIn first.

### Do I need my own LinkedIn developer app? 

Not when you connect through Markifact. Building directly on LinkedIn's Marketing APIs normally requires a developer application, product access, OAuth setup, and the permissions needed for advertising data or writes. Markifact provides the managed connection layer.

### Can one conversation work across multiple LinkedIn ad accounts? 

Yes. Markifact supports selecting and reusing multiple LinkedIn ad accounts, which is useful for agencies, regional teams, and businesses with separate product lines. You can compare results across accounts without reconnecting each one for every report.

### Can the AI change budgets or activate campaigns by itself? 

No write executes silently. The AI can prepare a budget, status, rename, campaign, ad set, or creative change, but Markifact stops the operation and presents an approval request before anything is sent to LinkedIn.

### How is MCP different from a LinkedIn Ads AI agent? 

MCP is the connection standard that exposes tools to an AI client. An AI agent is the reasoning layer that decides which tools to use and in what order. Markifact supplies the managed LinkedIn Ads tools, while your chosen assistant or agent handles the conversation and planning.

### How secure is the LinkedIn Ads connection? 

Markifact uses OAuth-based access, keeps LinkedIn tokens away from the AI client, and separates instant read operations from approval-gated writes. Account access follows the LinkedIn permissions granted during connection.

### Can I research competitors with LinkedIn's Ads Library? 

Yes. Markifact can search and retrieve public ads from LinkedIn's Ads Library, so an AI client can organize competitor messaging, offers, formats, and calls to action before you plan a campaign. See the dedicated LinkedIn Ads Library MCP page for supported filters, prompts, and examples.

### Are there open-source LinkedIn Ads MCP servers? 

Yes. By July 2026, community options included linkedin-campaign-manager-mcp, read-only projects such as CData's LinkedIn Ads MCP example, and broader multi-platform servers. Their authentication, write coverage, hosting model, safeguards, and maintenance vary, so review the code and LinkedIn API requirements before connecting an ad account.

### Is there a Claude connector for LinkedIn Ads? 

Yes. Markifact works as a LinkedIn Ads connector for Claude. A connector is Claude's name for an MCP server in its interface: add Markifact under Settings, then Connectors, enable the LinkedIn Ads tools, and Claude can pull reports and prepare approval-gated campaign changes. The same connector works in ChatGPT and other MCP clients.

### Can I also manage organic LinkedIn posts and my personal profile? 

Yes. Use the separate LinkedIn MCP for personal profile and Company Page analytics, publishing text, images, videos, and documents, and managing comments with approval before writes. Connect LinkedIn Organic in Markifact separately from your LinkedIn Ads account.
