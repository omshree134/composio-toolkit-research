# DealCloud MCP Server

The DealCloud MCP Server helps teams turn their DealCloud data into faster, higher-quality outcomes. AI assistants can surface sharper relationship and pipeline insights, prepare teams for diligence and meetings, then act on what they find: logging notes after a call, advancing a deal after a review, or recording a new opportunity. It all runs on a secure, consistent surface, with site and user-level permissions, governance, and attribution built in.

## How It Works

1. A compatible AI assistant connects to the DealCloud MCP Server and signs in as the user.
2. The assistant discovers the available tools and capabilities.
3. It calls read tools to retrieve context and surface relevant data.
4. It calls write tools to create or update records, respecting existing rules & validations
5. Every action and response honors individual users permissions.
6. Teams move from insight to action faster, with less manual work and DealCloud kept current.
