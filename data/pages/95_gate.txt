## Questions, answered.

### How does usage-based pricing work?

Reducto uses per-product pricing based on what you process. Standard rates start at $1 per 1,000 pages for r-1 mini Parse, $10 per 1,000 pages for r-1 Parse, and $20 per 1,000 pages for Extract, with parsing included. Standard accounts include $150 in free credits. For current rates and usage details, see our Usage and Pricing.
