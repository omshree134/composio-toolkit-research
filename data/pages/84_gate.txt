## Common Questions

What PCI compliance level does Paygent maintain?

Paygent is PCI DSS Level 1 certified, the highest level of compliance in the payments industry. When you use our tokenization APIs (Collect.js), your integration qualifies for SAQ-A, the simplest PCI self-assessment questionnaire.

Do you support ACH / eCheck payments?

Yes. ACH payment processing is available as a $25/month add-on. This includes both ACH debits (pulling funds from a customer's bank account) and ACH credits (pushing funds for payouts).

How fast are payouts / settlements?

Standard settlement is next-business-day (T+1). Same-day settlement is available for qualifying merchants. Tapn transactions settle within 48 hours.

Can I migrate from Stripe or Square?

Yes. We offer a white-glove migration service for merchants processing over $50K/month. We handle token migration, webhook re-mapping, and parallel testing.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. Both Paygent Platform and Tapn are month-to-month with no cancellation fees. Enterprise custom pricing may include annual commitments with volume discounts.

What payment methods do you support?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, JCB, UnionPay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, ACH/eCheck, and contactless NFC (via Tapn). Local payment methods are available in select regions.

Do you offer chargeback protection?

Paygent Radar provides AI-powered fraud scoring on every transaction. Combined with 3D Secure authentication and customizable risk rules, most fraud is blocked before it becomes a chargeback.

Is there a free trial?

We offer a sandbox environment with full API access at no cost. You can test transactions, webhooks, and integrations before going live. No credit card required to start.
