Ramp MCP

Ramp MCP connects AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor to your Ramp account. Each connection acts as the signed-in user, with that user's Ramp permissions.

## Connect

- Claude: Install Ramp from Claude's directory↗, then sign in to Ramp.
- ChatGPT: Install Ramp from the ChatGPT app directory↗.
- Cursor: Add Ramp to Cursor with one click.
- Claude Code:

Shell

Copy

claude mcp add --transport http ramp https://mcp.ramp.com/mcp

- Codex:

codex mcp add ramp --url https://mcp.ramp.com/mcp

- Other clients: Coding agents can also use Ramp CLI. For other MCP clients, gateways, and the sandbox, see Connect to Ramp↗.

When you sign in, choose Full access or Read-only. Connections expire after 30 days; reconnect to renew.

## Connection issues

- "The app you are using is not currently supported" means your client's redirect URI isn't allowlisted. Request a redirect URI.
- You can't connect at all: your Ramp role may not include access to AI tools. Ask your Admin; see Manage connected AI tools↗.

For the full tool list and more troubleshooting, see Connect to Ramp↗ and the Agent tools reference↗.
