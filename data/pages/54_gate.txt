## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans, billing, and getting started with MrScraper.

 What do I get on the Free plan? 

 Free costs nothing - no credit card required. It's the fastest way to try scraping, proxies and AI extraction before upgrading. 

 Can I change plans later? 

 Yes. Upgrade from Free to Essential anytime from your account's billing settings, or move to Enterprise by booking a call. 

 How do I get my API Token? 

 Sign in to the dashboard, open your account settings, and copy the token from the API Token page. Every API request authenticates with it, and the getting-started guide in the docs walks through your first call. 

 Can I use MrScraper without coding? 

 Yes. The visual scraper and AI Mode let you point at a page, describe the data you want, and get structured results without writing code. The API and SDKs are there when you want to automate further. 

 Why is my scrape not returning any data? 

 The usual causes are a page that loads its content with JavaScript, selectors that no longer match, or the site blocking the request. Re-run with rendering enabled, let AI Mode re-detect the fields, or route the run through the built-in proxies. 

 What’s the difference between AI Mode and Manual Mode? 

 AI Mode reads the page and extracts the fields you describe in plain language - the fastest way to a working scraper. Manual Mode gives you exact control with your own selectors and spends fewer tokens on predictable, high-volume runs. 

 When should I use a proxy? 

 Use one when a site limits how often one address can visit, or serves different content per country. Location-based proxies let you see each page as it appears in the region you choose. 

 Does MrScraper provide built-in proxies? 

 Yes. Residential proxies with country-level targeting are built into the platform - enable them per scraper, with no third-party setup. Bandwidth is billed separately from plan tokens, from $1.5/GB. 

 Can I schedule scrapers to run automatically? 

 Yes. Any scraper can run on a schedule, from once a minute to specific days of the week, and each run can deliver its results onward automatically. 

 Can I use MrScraper to track product reviews or sentiment? 

 Yes. Point a scraper at product or review pages and extract ratings, review text, and dates into structured rows, then schedule it so the dataset stays fresh for sentiment analysis. 

 How can I extract job listings with specific criteria? 

 Scrape the job board’s search or listing pages and describe the fields you need - title, company, location, salary. Filters in the target URL carry over, so the scraper only collects listings that match your criteria. 

 How can I lower my scraping costs? 

 Tokens are charged on the data a scrape extracts, so collect only the fields you need, prefer Manual Mode for stable high-volume jobs, and schedule runs at the frequency the data actually changes. 

 Can MrScraper send my data automatically? 

 Yes. Results can go straight to webhooks, SQL databases, AWS S3, Zapier, n8n, or email, so scraped data lands in your workflow without manual exports. 

 Why do some runs fail or hit rate limits? 

 Some sites limit how often they can be visited, and some only show their content once JavaScript has run. Enable the built-in residential proxies, lower the run frequency, and let the browser render the full page - most failed runs clear up with those three. 

 My scheduled scrapes didn’t run - what can I check? 

 Check that the schedule is still enabled, your token balance isn't exhausted (extra usage is $1 per 1,000 tokens), and the target page still matches the scraper's setup. The run history shows each attempt's result - if it still looks wrong, contact support from the dashboard.
