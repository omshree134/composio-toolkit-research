Higgsfield MCP connects Higgsfield to AI agents. Connect once and generate images, videos, characters, and audio from Claude or any MCP-compatible agent, without leaving your agent. The main difference from generating on the web: MCP always uses credits. Unlimited access and free generations apply only on higgsfield.ai, so anything generated through MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, or other automated tools deducts credits at standard rates.

## What is Higgsfield MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open protocol that lets AI agents connect to external services and call their tools. Higgsfield MCP is a connector that lets you generate images, videos, characters, and audio from Higgsfield directly inside Claude or another MCP-compatible agent. It isn't the Higgsfield API: MCP connects to your existing account and uses your existing plan credits, with no API key required. For building generation into your own product with per-request billing, see What is the Higgsfield API, and how do I get started?

Because MCP connects programmatically, using Higgsfield through it falls under the Developer Terms in the Terms of Use. In short: keep your connection secure, and you're responsible for anything an agent generates through your account.

MCP connector URL: https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp

## How is MCP different from the web?

The most important difference is credit behavior: everything generated through MCP deducts credits, regardless of your plan.

How is MCP different from the web?

| Criteria | Web (higgsfield.ai) | MCP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unlimited model access | Yes, where included in plan | No, always deducts credits |
| Free generations | Yes, where included in plan | No |
| Credit cost | Standard rates | Standard rates |
| Generations appear in Assets | Yes | Yes |
| Access to models | Yes | Yes |

## Do Unlimited models and free generations work through MCP?

No. Unlimited access and free generations apply only on higgsfield.ai: they're intended for manual use, not for automated or programmatic generation. Anything generated through MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, or other automated tools deducts credits at standard rates. To generate on a model where your plan includes Unlimited access without spending credits, do it on the web at higgsfield.ai.

## What can I do through MCP?

- Image and video generation across the Higgsfield model lineup, with results saved to your Assets.
- Marketing Studio: paste a product link and get ready video ads built on a set of presets.
- Ad Multiplier: turn a reference ad and your product or character images into new versions of the same ad with a different product, location, hook, or CTA.
- Genjutsu: Motion Transfer, Object Swap, and Restyle for video.
- Upscale: raise the resolution and quality of your images and videos. How Upscale works on the website is covered in How do I use Upscale?
- Soul characters: train a character from photos and reuse it across generations.
- Higgsfield Audio and credit balance checks.
- Asset Library: browse your past generations and use them as references.

For the full list of operations and how to phrase requests to your agent, see How do I connect Higgsfield to Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI agent?

## Got any questions left?

Do I need a separate API key to use Higgsfield MCP?

No. MCP connects through OAuth authorization, no API key is needed. API keys belong to the Higgsfield API, a separate developer product. See What is the Higgsfield API, and how do I get started?

I have Unlimited access to a model, but MCP charged credits for it. Is that a bug?

No, that's expected. Unlimited access and free generations apply only on higgsfield.ai, so generating through MCP always uses credits at standard rates.

Does MCP work in other agents besides Claude?

Yes. Any MCP-compatible agent can connect using the same server URL: https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp. In ChatGPT, use the official Higgsfield Plugin instead: see How do I connect Higgsfield to Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI agent? Third-party agents are governed by their own terms, and you're responsible for how they use your Higgsfield account.

Where do my MCP generations appear?

In your Assets on higgsfield.ai, tagged with the MCP source. For details and troubleshooting, see How do I connect Higgsfield to Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI agent?
