## Transparent AI pricing

LiveAgent doesn't charge for AI. You connect your own AI provider, such as OpenAI, Anthropic or FlowHunt, and pay it for what you use.

### Who you pay

You pay LiveAgent

$0 for AI

Just your plan price. No AI fees, no token markup, no per-resolution fees.

Every AI feature included Same price however much AI you use

You pay your AI provider

Only what you use

Billed directly by the provider you choose, at their prices. Switch providers any time.

- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Google Gemini
- FlowHunt

FlowHunt is our own AI platform, with ready-made templates for your use cases. Whichever AI provider you choose, we set it up for free.

### What your plan counts

#### AI agents

An AI agent is an AI worker with its own instructions, AI model and permissions. It can chat with visitors, reply to tickets and sort them.

Your plan sets how many AI agents you can create: Starter 1, Team 5, Business 50, Enterprise unlimited.

One AI agent can work in many places, for example the same chatbot on three websites. It still counts once.

### Built in, no AI agent needed

These run on your AI provider too, but they don't use an AI agent, so they don't count toward your LiveAgent plan.

- Summaries
- Call transcripts
- Translations

## Frequently asked questions

### Black Friday

#### How do I get the Black Friday price?

Choose a plan and pay by December 15, 2026 with the code for your billing: BLACKFRIDAY40Y takes 40% off annual billing for the first year, BLACKFRIDAY33M takes 33% off monthly billing for the first 12 bills. After that, your plan's regular price applies. It works on every plan, Enterprise included, for new and existing customers. The discount applies to the plan and billing period you pay for with the code.

### Plans and billing

#### Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial, and no credit card is required. When the trial ends, upgrade to a paid plan to keep your account and everything you set up.

#### Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, except in the 24 hours before your billing day. If you cancel, your account stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

#### Can my price go up?

No. Your plan keeps its regular price for as long as you keep the plan, also when you add agents. A discount you get with a code ends when its period is over. We may change the prices of our plans, but a new price applies only to new customers and to customers who change plan. Moving up within 12 months? Ask us for a special quote.

#### Is there a contract or a setup fee?

No setup fees and no long-term commitment: pay monthly and cancel anytime, or pay annually and pay less per agent. Buying through procurement? With an annual plan we provide a written quote and a contract: request a quote.

#### How does LiveAgent's price compare with Zendesk?

Our Zendesk pricing guide puts Zendesk's current plans and add-ons next to LiveAgent's. Moving over is free: see Zendesk migration.

#### Can you help me migrate from another help desk?

Yes, free on every paid plan: our built-in importers move tickets, contacts and the knowledge base from Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout and other help desks. Large accounts can take several hours or even days to import. How migration works.

#### Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, American Express, Diners), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and wire transfer on annual plans. Prices are in USD and exclude VAT. Customers in the EU are invoiced in EUR. VAT is added where it applies: an EU company with a valid VAT ID outside Slovakia pays no VAT (reverse charge) when it enters the VAT ID in its account.

#### What do phone calls cost?

LiveAgent charges nothing for calls. Your call center runs on your own phone provider, which bills you directly for phone numbers and minutes.

#### Do you offer a startup discount?

Early-stage startups can apply for a special startup deal.

#### How much do I save with annual billing?

Up to 21% per agent; how much depends on the plan. Switch between monthly and annual billing above the plans: each card shows your team's monthly total for that billing.

#### How is Enterprise priced?

A flat monthly fee that includes 80 agents, about $50 per agent (about $40 on annual billing). Extra agents are $49 / month, or $39 / month on annual billing.

#### What is the difference between Business and Enterprise?

Business is billed per agent, for any team size, with higher limits than Team. Enterprise is a flat fee with 80 agents included, the highest limits and a dedicated account manager.

### AI features

#### How much does AI cost?

A typical AI chat costs about $0.11 at your AI provider, so 300 chats a month cost about $33–$81 (at our median and our average); LiveAgent adds nothing on top. $0.11 is the median in LiveAgent's own support in October 2026 (how we measured it, with the average and our newest chat flow). Drafting replies with AI Answer Assistant costs about $2–3 per 1,000 replies with a low-cost model such as Claude Haiku (our estimate from the providers' list prices, October 2026). The cost depends on the model you choose, the tools an AI agent uses and the length of the chat.

#### Do I need an AI provider account?

Yes. AI features run on your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or FlowHunt account, and you pay the provider directly, only for what you use. Your AI provider processes the text under your agreement with it.

#### Does LiveAgent charge for AI?

No. No AI fees, no token markup, no per-resolution fees: you pay only your AI provider, for what you use. For AI, plans differ only in the number of AI agents.

#### What if I need more AI agents than my plan includes?

You can move to a higher plan. Enterprise includes unlimited AI agents.

#### What is the MCP server on Business and Enterprise?

On every plan, your AI agents use LiveAgent's built-in tools. On Business and Enterprise, you can also connect external AI tools, such as Claude, to LiveAgent through its MCP server.

#### Are there any usage limits?

Each plan except Enterprise has a limit on the number of AI agents. Where a plan says "unlimited", there is no plan limit, but to keep LiveAgent fast and reliable for everyone, we apply technical and rate limits to AI agents, the API and the MCP server to prevent misuse.
