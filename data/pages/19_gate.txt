# 1. Register on Developer Portal & Get Gorgias Sandbox Account

- Register on our Developer Portal;
- Once you register, feel free to create a trial account and reach out to techpartners@gorgias.com to transform it into a developer account. 

- ⚠️ You will need to have a draft app to get approved for the Sandbox Account

> ### 👉
> 
> Currently all new Sandbox accounts are Demo accounts by default. Once you get access to your account, please reach out to techpartners@gorgias.com with your account URL to switch it to a Developer plan. We appreciate your patience while we are working on automating this process!
